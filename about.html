About North Star Bakery

Our story

North Star Bakery began with a simple goal: create a welcoming neighborhood place where fresh bread and familiar treats bring people together. What started as a small morning bake has grown into a community bakery serving commuters, families, neighbors, and local events.

We still work in small batches and keep the counter personal. We want every visit to feel warm, useful, and connected to the neighborhood around us.

Our sourcing philosophy

We choose ingredients for flavor, consistency, and responsible use. When practical, we work with regional flour suppliers, seasonal fruit growers, and nearby producers so our menu can reflect the ingredients available around us.

We also plan our daily bake carefully to reduce unnecessary waste while keeping customer favorites available throughout the day.
